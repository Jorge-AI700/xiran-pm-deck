6. Risks & Mitigations

Risks & Mitigations

Risk 01 · Technical DMS & Data Fragmentation

Mexican car dealerships run heterogeneous local DMSs and spreadsheets, risking slow onboarding.

Mitigation: Standalone 6-tab portal + standardized $1,800 data-mapping onboarding (no fragile DMS write-access needed on Day 1).
Risk 02 · Adoption Caller Skepticism & Edge Cases

Complex diagnostic complaints or frustrated callers can erode trust if an AI bot stalls.

Mitigation: Natural es-MX voice + strict code guardrails + 1-click warm transfer to human service/renewal advisors.
Risk 03 · Compliance Outbound Renewal Fatigue

Unsolicited outbound calls risk low pickup rates or opt-out complaints.

Mitigation: Dial only existing dealership policyholders within the 30-day renewal window, capped at 3 attempts with instant opt-out logging.
